More to Explore in
Downtown Los Angeles

Architecture is only part of the experience. Downtown LA also offers food, markets, public spaces, and cultural attractions that make it easy to turn a visit into a full day trip.

Why Explore Downtown LA

Entertainment
Beyond the Buildings

Downtown Los Angeles offers more than architecture and history. It is also home to lively food halls, open-air markets, public gathering spaces, and other experiences that give people more reasons to stay, explore, and enjoy the area.

What you can do,
and what to notice
while you're there.

Each of these places carries details most visitors walk past. Here's where to begin.

Grand Central
Market

Historic Food Hall

Why visit

Grand Central Market is one of Downtown Los Angeles's most recognizable destinations. It brings together a wide range of food vendors and gives visitors a lively place to experience the city's energy and variety. The historic atmosphere, busy crowds, local food options, and the mix of old and new Downtown Los Angeles.

Smorgasburg Los
Angeles

Open-Air Food Market

Why visit

Smorgasburg Los Angeles offers a different side of Downtown LA through food, shopping, and live energy. The open-air layout and wide mix of vendors make it feel like an event as much as a market. The outdoor setting, the variety of vendors, and the social atmosphere that makes it a destination on its own.

LA Live

Sports & Entertainment Complex

Why visit

L.A. Live provides a high-energy contrast to the historic architecture of Downtown LA. Anchored by major sports arenas and world-class event venues, this modern complex brings together dining, nightlife, and entertainment in one concentrated area. The sleek design, neon lights, and vibrant plazas create a dynamic metropolitan atmosphere.
